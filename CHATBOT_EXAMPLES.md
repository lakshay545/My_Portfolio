## 💡 Pro Tips for Users

**Ask For:**
- ✅ "Tell me about your projects" (specific)
- ✅ "Show me your GitHub" (specific)
- ✅ "How can I contact you?" (specific)
- ✅ "What hackathons have you done?" (specific)

**Don't Ask For:**
- ❌ "umm" (too vague)
- ❌ "help" (too vague)
- ❌ "Weather" (off-topic)

**Result:** The chatbot will give you the most relevant, detailed response!
